# Foreword

Open electrical-engineering courses are not scarce. The slow part is deciding what remains usable: whether the assignments have feedback, which software and instruments are assumed, and how one course differs from another with nearly the same title.

EEDIY puts those decisions on the course page. Each guide aims to identify the official entry point, public notes and assignments, solutions or another feedback route, prerequisites, tool and hardware requirements, and the scope an independent learner can honestly complete. Neighboring courses are compared directly so that the learner does not have to reconstruct the difference across a dozen tabs.

The navigation and course-page reading model come directly from [CSDIY](https://csdiy.wiki/). The EE content is checked afresh because devices, instruments, EDA licensing, and laboratory safety can change whether a course is genuinely usable.

When choosing a course, open its first substantial assignment. Access to the work, feedback, and required tools usually matters more than the polish of the course homepage. Choose one mainline for a topic; use an alternative or supplement when access, prerequisites, or teaching style creates a specific problem.

## Laboratory access is a prerequisite too

A broken program can usually be rerun. A mistaken connection to mains, a battery, a laser, RF power, or rotating machinery may fail in a very different way. A laboratory appearing in a syllabus does not make it suitable for home reproduction. Work involving mains voltage, high voltage or current, high-energy storage, intense light, RF exposure, chemicals, or fabrication equipment belongs in compliant facilities with qualified supervision. When those conditions are missing, stop at analysis, simulation, public data, or a genuinely low-energy teaching platform.

!!! danger "Read the safety guide before applying power"
    The [Laboratory Safety guide](guides/safety.md) distinguishes work that can proceed, work that should move to simulation, and work that needs a qualified facility. It does not replace equipment manuals, laboratory procedure, or local rules.

## Find one question and begin

Choose the course nearest to the problem in front of you, verify its first assignment, feedback route, and tools, then complete one small task that an answer, test, simulation, or measurement can correct. Report dead links, edition changes, and missing judgments through the focused feedback links below; use the [contribution entry point](contributing.md) when you have evidence and a concrete edit.
