### Modeling exercise

Build a computation-only notebook with at least 2 chains: generate an interferogram from spectrum, path difference, and degree of coherence; and obtain detector current/SNR from photon flux, quantum efficiency, and dark/shot/thermal noise. Add 1 model from three/four-level gain, laser rate equations, fiber normalized frequency, or a Malus/Pockels transfer. Every model needs units, domain, analytical baseline, parameter sweep, and 1 out-of-domain failure.

Check zero path difference and the short-coherence limit for the
interferogram; zero power and high-photon-flux noise trends for the detector;
and Jones norm plus Malus \(\cos^2\theta\) for polarization. Put raw arrays,
environment, and the rerun command beside the notebook.

This route is viewing, derivation, and simulation only; do not recreate EDFA,
fiber-laser, EOM, Michelson, or bare-fiber experiments at home. End with one
source-to-detector chain: use rays for optical geometry, switch to
wave/coherence for fringe visibility, then switch to photon/carrier variables
at the detector. The revealing point is the handoff. If one model's output
cannot become the next model's input for the same observable, a list of device
names has not yet become a photonics system.
