## Read Both Versions of the Prerequisite Story

The formal
[Fall 2022 course information](https://manipulation.csail.mit.edu/Fall2022/index.html)
lists basic linear algebra, probability, algorithms, basic neural networks,
and Python cloud-Jupyter assignments. Its FAQ also says that prior robotics
experience is unnecessary and calls basic familiarity with linear algebra the
only required prerequisite. The actual entry check should therefore stay with
basic linear algebra: multiply matrices, solve a small least-squares problem,
and manage shapes and broadcasting in Python/NumPy. Probability, algorithms,
and neural networks make later units easier, but they are not grounds for
excluding a learner at the start.

A homogeneous-transform example, a Jacobian, and a linearly constrained QP
are useful stretch previews. They are not admission tests. Kinematics and
trajectory optimization are taught in the course, including the optimization
ideas they need. If both Python and linear algebra are weak, Drake exceptions
become difficult to distinguish from geometry errors; strengthen either layer
first.

## Ten Problem Sets Grow One Software Stack

The
[Fall 2022 schedule](https://manipulation.csail.mit.edu/Fall2022/schedule.html)
lists Problem Sets 1–10: Simulation; Kinematics I and II; Geometry I; Geometry
II and Grasping; Simulation and Behavior; Deep Perception; Planning;
Control/Project Check-in; and Reinforcement Learning. The first half is not a
set of isolated demos. It progressively adds interfaces across Drake,
MeshCat, kinematics, point clouds, grasping, state machines, planning, and
control.

An independent route can use four blocks:

1. PS1–PS3 cover simulation, frames/poses, Jacobians, trajectories, and
   optimization; record units, coordinates, and notebook outputs first.
2. PS4–PS5 add registration, outliers, free-space constraints, clutter, and
   grasping; preserve point-cloud inputs and failed overlays.
3. PS6–PS8 connect behavior, deep perception, and motion planning; compare
   algorithms on fixed scenes instead of saving only a successful animation.
4. PS9–PS10 connect force/manipulator control, a project check-in, and RL,
   then see whether the notebooks rerun on the compatibility baseline below.

Problem pages are public, but written answers, Piazza, Canvas, Gradescope
feedback, and the enrolled autograder are not equivalently available to an
outside learner. Deepnote also requires an account. Local tests should check
transform orthogonality, collision-free status, termination, and deterministic
seeds, while recording the missing course feedback. The information page
announces a midterm and no final exam, but the public schedule supplies no
midterm paper or solution. This is not a complete exam archive.

## The Final Project Must Report Scene Distributions, Collisions, and Solvers

The
[project page](https://manipulation.csail.mit.edu/Fall2022/project.html)
allocates 4–5 weeks and permits teams of 2–3. Reimplementing a paper or
applying an existing method to manipulation is acceptable; novelty is not
mandatory. The preproposal is about 500–750 words and the final proposal about
1000 words. Final videos are 3, 4, or 5 minutes for one-, two-, or
three-person projects, and the suggested report length is 1500–4500 words with
abstract, related work, methods, results, discussion, and contributions. For
6.4210, assignments are 40%, proposal 10%, report 30%, video 10%,
journal/peer review 5%, and CI-M participation 5%. Writing is not decoration
added after the code.

I would choose a simulation-only task from the official ideas, such as clutter
clearing, peg-in-hole, or a motion-planner benchmark. Define a distribution
over initial poses, occlusions, masses, and friction, then report success,
collision/minimum distance, solver termination, and runtime by scene group.
A video can show representative trajectories but cannot replace collision
statistics. Isolate cases sensitive to optimization initialization. Finish
with a sim-to-real table for calibration, latency, contact, and sensing, even
when no robot is used. Clearly separate upstream code from the learner’s
changes.

retain broad ranges from `pyproject.toml`. This is neither the Fall 2022
classroom environment nor a fully locked 2026 environment.

No transitive dependency lock or published clean-install record accompanies
this guide. Install in a clean virtual environment and run a minimal
smoke-test notebook, recording OS, Python, Drake, manipulation commit, solver,
and any error. That tests one machine; it does not turn the suggested baseline
into a verified lock. Deepnote was the course platform, but cloud images and
project links can change; export notebooks and preserve dependency
information. Some `MathematicalProgram` exercises may use academically
licensed solvers. Where
an exercise permits an open solver, record the substitution, termination,
objective, and constraint residual. Results from different solvers are not
automatically equivalent.

## Simulation, Deepnote, and Hardware Do Not Demonstrate the Same Thing

The course says that class hardware was available for ambitious final projects
while cloud simulation was heavily used. That does not give an outside reader
access to a KUKA iiwa, Franka, gripper, camera, driver computers, or staff
supervision. The current text’s `HardwareStation` also exposes ground-truth
“cheat ports” that exist only in simulation; a real
`HardwareStationInterface` does not. An evaluated controller must not read
those ports. They may be used only for offline labels and error analysis.

A passing simulation demonstrates behavior under a fixed model, parameters,
and solver. It does not establish real-hardware calibration, latency, contact,
friction, sensing, or safety. The independent route requires no robot purchase,
and much of kinematics/planning runs on a CPU. Bound deep-perception or RL
cost with small data, fixed training budgets, and cached results. Physical arm
work belongs in a qualified lab with risk assessment, workspace exclusion,
speed/force limits, emergency stop, collision protection, and supervision.
The outside-study record can contain outputs from ten public problem sets and
a simulation project. It does not demonstrate safe home operation or recreate
the original class hardware conditions.
