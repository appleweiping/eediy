### Course format

MIT [6.452 Spring 2006](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-452-principles-of-wireless-communications-spring-2006/)
covers fading, diversity, power control, MIMO, multiuser/broadcast channels,
OFDM, and UWB. The
[syllabus](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-452-principles-of-wireless-communications-spring-2006/pages/syllabus/)
requires 6.450 or instructor permission plus digital communications,
probability, and linear algebra. It fits learners who already know AWGN,
matched filters, and modulation and want to study channel, CSI, and power
assumptions through Tse–Viswanath. It is not a standalone main course when
continuous notes, videos, and solved work are required.

### The Material Granularity Determines the Method

The [assignments](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-452-principles-of-wireless-communications-spring-2006/pages/assignments/)
contain only 3 problem sets without solutions and rely heavily on the text.
For each problem, state channel model, CSI at transmitter/receiver, power
constraint, metric, and limiting case. Fix matrix rank and dimension for MIMO
comparisons; distinguish ergodic capacity, outage, and BER for fading. A
Rayleigh-outage or 2×2 MIMO Monte Carlo can serve as an independent numerical
check, but it is not an official MIT lab.

### The Original Project Is a Paper Review

The [project page](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-452-principles-of-wireless-communications-spring-2006/pages/projects/)
describes a paper review, and the
[readings](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-452-principles-of-wireless-communications-spring-2006/pages/readings/)
provide candidates without rubric, code, data, or feedback. A review should
state the problem and baseline, channel assumptions, central result, method,
and limitation, then recalculate a figure or special case. If proprietary
data are unavailable, use a synthetic channel and place the authors' setting
beside the synthetic one.

End with an assumption transplant: change a CSI, fading, or power condition,
predict which conclusion will move, and check the direction with a small
model. Spring 2006 UWB and system examples are version-bound; use a current
primary source for modern standards. Label the authors' result, your
recalculation, and a conceptual sketch separately. Beside a synthetic channel,
state which statistics remain and which temporal or spatial
correlations it discards. The useful result is not a similar-looking curve but
an answer to what the conclusion depends on—transmitter CSI, the fading law,
or the power constraint. Changing any one can reverse the engineering choice.
