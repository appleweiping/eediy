### Prerequisites

NPTEL [Analog IC Design](https://nptel.ac.in/courses/108106105) is a 12-week
IIT Madras course. Before starting, be able to find a MOS operating point,
draw a small-signal model, analyze common-source and differential stages, and
reason about stability from loop gain; ideal op-amps alone are insufficient.
The official [syllabus](https://archive.nptel.ac.in/content/syllabus_pdf/108106105.pdf)
connects MOS review, noise and mismatch, feedback and compensation,
single/two-stage op-amps, telescopic and folded cascodes, fully differential
design, and CMFB.
Choose it when bias, small-signal analysis, and feedback already coexist in
your design record; otherwise use a preparatory course.

Use the [2018 archived preview](https://onlinecourses-archive.nptel.ac.in/noc18_ec05/preview) as the stable reference.
The current [NOC26-EE66](https://onlinecourses.nptel.ac.in/noc26_ee66/preview)
governs only the 2026 service, deadlines, and certificate. Do not combine the
two runs.

### Fix Gain, GBW, and Swing before Comparing Topologies

Compare every structure on gain, GBW, phase margin, slew, output swing,
common-mode range, noise, power, load, and mismatch. Build a hand budget
before SPICE, and hold supply, current, load, and device model constant across
candidates. Count compensation in a two-stage design against area, slew, and
settling rather than displaying only the final winner.

The 2018 [announcements](https://onlinecourses-archive.nptel.ac.in/noc18_ec05/announcements)
record Assignment 0 and 12 weekly assignments. Solutions were released for
most weeks, but Assignment 12 lacks equivalent support and the historical
grader may not run. Attempt first; review the unsupported week with a text,
peer, or simulation.

### A Complete Op-Amp Design Exposes the Tradeoffs

Given supply, load, DC gain, UGB, phase margin, slew, input/output range,
noise, and power, compare at least 2 topologies. Separate testbenches should
cover DC, differential and common-mode AC, loop stability, large-signal step,
slew, settling, load and supply sweeps, and noise. A fully differential design
also needs a separate CMFB check for sensing range, target common mode,
startup, and stability.

Noise and mismatch cannot disappear after their introductory weeks. Build an
input-referred budget, distinguish thermal and \(1/f\) noise and systematic
versus random mismatch, and state the integration bandwidth. If the model has
no statistical parameters, leave the result unverified rather than inventing
a process distribution.

The public course includes no PDK, layout, DRC/LVS, or post-layout extraction.
With a generic model, matching and parasitics remain unverified; without
statistical parameters, Monte Carlo has no credible input. The meaningful
result is an explanation of how constraints eliminate architectures; 12
watched weeks alone do not constitute an IC implementation.

Preserve one rejected topology and its failure reason. If every candidate is
represented only by successful curves, the trade among gain, headroom, power,
stability, and settling has disappeared from the design record.

The last constraint table should connect each specification to a calculation,
simulation, or explicit unknown. When two topologies both meet nominal
targets, corners, settling behavior, and implementation complexity—not the
prettier curve—should decide between them.
