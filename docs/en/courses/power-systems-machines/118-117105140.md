### The syllabus and video index jointly define the scope

IIT Kharagpur's [Power System Analysis](https://nptel.ac.in/courses/117105140), taught by Debapriya Das, is positioned as a 3rd-year undergraduate electrical-engineering course. It is not a first survey of the grid. Learners should already know three-phase circuits, complex power, transformer equivalent circuits, linear algebra, and basic numerical methods. Before starting, convert impedances across a transformer on a consistent MVA/kV base and form \(Y_{bus}\) from branch data.

### Fix One MVA/kV Base before the Three Analyses

The steady-state thread begins with line and component models, forms \(Y_{bus}\), distinguishes PQ, PV, and slack buses, and solves Gauss–Seidel or Newton–Raphson load flow. Preserve bus types, initialization, mismatch norms, PV reactive limits, and line-loss balance with each result. “Converged” alone does not establish correct signs, limits, or network data.

The fault thread builds positive-, negative-, and zero-sequence networks, connects them for the specified fault, and reconstructs phase quantities. The common failure is often inconsistent base conversion or sequence connection, not matrix arithmetic. The stability thread takes its operating point from the pre-fault load flow and develops a single-machine infinite-bus swing equation. Report the integration step and add an energy, equal-area, or step-convergence check. The causal link among all three threads should remain visible.

### Use a 3–5-Bus System to Check Load Flow, Faults, and Stability

Choose a synthetic or clearly licensed teaching network and preserve its one-line diagram, bus and branch data, base table, and assumptions. Complete load flow, a 3-phase and an unbalanced fault, then feed the same operating point into a swing simulation. Check load-flow residuals against a 2-bus hand calculation or another implementation, verify fault sequence connections and phase reconstruction, and sweep clearing time and integration step for stability. The final artifact should expose the chain from pre-fault state to fault-time electrical quantities and post-clearing motion, not present three unrelated screenshots.

[pandapower](https://github.com/e2nIEE/pandapower), developed by the University
of Kassel and Fraunhofer IEE teams, is BSD-3-Clause software with power-flow,
unbalanced, and short-circuit notebooks. It is neither an NPTEL dataset nor a
lab. Pin the base, element tables, package version, and the same 3–5-bus
system, then compare bus voltages, mismatch, and fault current. Model the
swing simulation separately; steady-state software output is not dynamic
validation.

This is a numerical project, not an official NPTEL lab or operational advice for a real grid. Do not use confidential topology, relay settings, or unauthorized SCADA or PMU data. Faults and stability are model objects here; physical work involving mains, three-phase sources, transformers, switchgear, or fault injection belongs only in an institutionally approved laboratory under qualified supervision.
