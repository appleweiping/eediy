### 6.243J Asks Where Linear Conclusions Fail

Choose MIT [6.243J](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-243j-dynamics-of-nonlinear-systems-fall-2003/) only after linear control is secure and you want to know where local conclusions fail; PID tuning alone is too early. This Fall 2003 graduate course moves from flows, equilibria, and linearization through Lyapunov/storage functions, input-output stability, bifurcation, feedback linearization, backstepping, and adaptive control. The [Syllabus](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-243j-dynamics-of-nonlinear-systems-fall-2003/pages/syllabus/) assumes 6.241 or equivalent linear control.

A two-dimensional vector field makes the prerequisites concrete: locate equilibria, calculate the Jacobian, draw nullclines, explain why linearization is silent at a non-hyperbolic point, and try 1 Lyapunov candidate. ODE existence/uniqueness, quadratic forms, and the chain rule should already be reliable.

### Read lecture notes with counterexamples

The [Lecture Notes](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-243j-dynamics-of-nonlinear-systems-fall-2003/pages/lecture-notes/) are the core. Label every theorem local/global, autonomous/time-varying, and stability/asymptotic/exponential, then pair it with 1 satisfying example and 1 near-miss. A few trajectories do not establish global stability, and an inconclusive Jacobian does not establish instability.

For phase portraits, level sets, and parameter sweeps, preserve solver, tolerance, initial-condition grid, and events. Predict with an invariant or \(\dot V\) before using plots to seek counterexamples. Trajectories crossing discontinuities or stiff regions also require verification.

Choose an equilibrium where linearization gives a weak answer, then compare it with an energy function and direct integration. When linearization establishes only local stability, enlarge initial conditions along different directions until a trajectory first leaves the estimated attraction region, then compare that boundary with a Lyapunov sublevel set. This exposes the gap between “the simulation appears to converge” and “the theorem covers this initial state.”

### Assignments progress from phase portraits to nonlinear design

[Assignments](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-243j-dynamics-of-nonlinear-systems-fall-2003/pages/assignments/) includes PDFs and some `.m` files, covering ODEs/invariant sets, linearization, Lyapunov/storage, bifurcation, input-output properties, feedback linearization/backstepping, and adaptive ideas. State the region of validity and origin of each candidate before checking positivity, derivative sign, radial unboundedness, and the largest invariant set.

The 2003 MATLAB/Simulink, Control Systems, and LMI toolbox environment needs adaptation. Preserve the requested quantity and record replacement functions, solver, version, tolerance, and constraint residual. Do not remove the computational part merely because an old `.m` file is difficult to run.

### Synthesis exercise

[Exams](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-243j-dynamics-of-nonlinear-systems-fall-2003/pages/exams/) supplies 2 take-home quizzes with 24-hour windows. In the original course, homework was 50% and each quiz 25%, with no final. Work within normal sleep hours, then classify corrections as candidate choice, local/global confusion, missed invariant conditions, or numerical overreach.

Use a pendulum or saturated second-order plant: begin with open-loop phase portraits, add local feedback, feedback linearization or backstepping, then test input saturation and parameter error. State the convergence region, peak control, steady-state error, a failing initial condition, and a counterexample index. Without persistent excitation, an adaptive design may claim tracking-error behavior, not identified parameters.

The case should also identify where the closed loop is well-defined: when a feedback-linearization decoupling term approaches singularity, how a backstepping virtual control enters the following recursion, and why saturation changes the sign argument for the Lyapunov derivative. For one failing initial condition, decide whether to change the controller or narrow the valid region. That decision is exactly where 6.243J departs from a linear-control course.
