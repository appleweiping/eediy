### Course fit

Cornell's [ECE 5760 page](https://people.ece.cornell.edu/land/courses/ece5760/)
currently anchors Spring 2026 *Hardware Acceleration via FPGA* on the
DE1-SoC/Cyclone V with Quartus 18.1. The site also retains 2020 videos, 2017
handouts, and older DE2/Nios II archives, but they belong to different
platform generations. Fix the record to “Spring 2026 + DE1-SoC + Quartus
18.1” and label every archival borrowing.

The course fits learners who already know C, synthesizable Verilog,
memory-mapped I/O, fixed-point arithmetic, and basic parallel algorithms. Its
subject is the division of work between the ARM HPS and FPGA fabric; an online
HDL simulator cannot stand in for SDRAM, VGA, audio, and HPS bridges. Review
digital design and architecture first if a valid/ready interface with
backpressure is not yet comfortable.

### Assignments and feedback

The Spring 2026 sequence is an HPS-controlled hardware ODE solver, a
[Mandelbrot visualizer](https://vanhunteradams.com/DE1/Mandelbrot/Mandelbrot.html),
[multiprocessor drum synthesis](https://vanhunteradams.com/DE1/Drum/Drum_synthesis.html),
and a final project. The first 3 train fixed-point state updates, pixel
parallelism, discrete wave models, and audio timing while repeatedly using the
HPS/FPGA interface.

Each needs a software reference, RTL testbench, Quartus timing/resource
report, and either board measurements or an explicitly simulation-only result.
ODE and Mandelbrot work should state widths, overflow, and termination.
Drum synthesis should connect sample rate and compute cadence to the output.
An image or sound is a demonstration, not a numerical comparison.

### Read Interfaces from Complete Examples

The Quartus 18.1 [design-example index](https://people.ece.cornell.edu/land/courses/ece5760/DE1_SOC/HPS_peripherials/Examples_version_18.html)
publishes complete ZIPs. The Mandelbrot
[HPS C source](https://people.ece.cornell.edu/land/courses/ece5760/DE1_SOC/HPS_peripherials/version_18_1_examples/Mandelbrot/mandelbrot_video_fix.c),
an audio [MATLAB script](https://people.ece.cornell.edu/land/courses/ece5760/DE1_SOC/HPS_FPGA/master_peripheral/EBAB_Audio/EBAB_and_HPS/UDP_audio_thread.m),
and the string-synthesis
[Verilog/Quartus project](https://people.ece.cornell.edu/land/courses/ece5760/DE1_SOC/DSP/Audio/Synthesis/Improved_string/verilog_improved_string.zip)
expose the path through the HPS process, bridge, memory, and peripheral.

Preserve an untouched project and its build report before changing one
parameter at a time. The top entity, target, pins, clock, Platform Designer
system, and register addresses must belong to one version. A project that
opens may have stale constraints, and synthesis does not establish timing
closure.

### Frame the Final Project as a Performance Question

Use a falsifiable target: frame rate at fixed resolution and clock, a
processing-element count under a resource budget, or acceleration over a
software baseline. The proposal should state the block diagram, I/O,
fixed-point plan, verification strategy, and resource/bandwidth estimates.
The result should identify whether computation, memory, a bridge, or I/O was
the bottleneck.

The public site supplies no DE1-SoC, Cornell workstation, in-person sign-off,
or submission system. Without a board, a learner can complete the reference
model, simulation, synthesis, and performance model, but should not call that
HPS/board validation. Put the stable operating range, resource margin, and
known failures beside the performance curves. A picture or sound becomes evidence
of an accelerator only when a frame-rate or throughput change can be traced to
compute, memory, the bridge, or I/O rather than merely to the demonstration.
