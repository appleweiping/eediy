# Community conduct / 社区行为准则

EEDIY welcomes learners and contributors across experience levels, languages,
regions, institutions, identities, and hardware budgets.

EEDIY 欢迎不同经验、语言、地区、院校、身份与硬件预算的学习者和贡献者。

## Expected behaviour / 我们期待

- Be specific, patient, and constructive.
- Critique evidence, designs, and code without attacking people.
- Respect different access constraints and avoid treating expensive equipment
  or institutional affiliation as a measure of ability.
- Credit sources and collaborators.
- Protect privacy, credentials, unpublished work, and safety-sensitive details.
- Correct mistakes openly and make room for questions.

请具体、耐心、建设性地沟通；批评证据、设计与代码，而不是攻击个人；尊重不同的
访问条件，不把昂贵设备或院校背景当作能力标准；注明来源与协作者；保护隐私、
凭证、未公开成果与安全敏感信息；公开纠错，并为提问保留空间。

## Unacceptable behaviour / 不可接受

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
misinformation, plagiarism, credential sharing, unsafe experimentation
pressure, and sustained disruption are not accepted in project spaces.

骚扰、歧视、威胁、性化关注、人肉搜索、故意传播错误信息、剽窃、共享凭证、施压
他人进行不安全实验，以及持续干扰项目协作，均不可接受。

## Reporting and response / 报告与处理

For ordinary conduct concerns, open a moderation issue without including
private or dangerous details. For sensitive cases, ask a maintainer for a
private reporting channel. Maintainers will limit disclosure, preserve relevant
evidence, hear affected parties where safe, and choose a proportionate response
such as clarification, warning, temporary restriction, or removal.

普通行为问题可提交 moderation issue，但不要包含隐私或危险细节。敏感情况请先向
维护者请求私密报告渠道。维护者将限制信息传播、保留必要证据，在安全前提下听取
相关方意见，并采取澄清、警告、临时限制或移除等适度措施。
