# Governance / 治理

EEDIY is maintained as a public learning commons. Decisions optimize for
learner safety, factual traceability, self-study executability, accessibility,
and long-term maintainability.

EEDIY 作为公共学习资源维护。决策优先考虑学习者安全、事实可追踪、自学可执行、
无障碍与长期可维护性。

## Decision process / 决策流程

- Factual corrections supported by primary evidence take priority.
- Curriculum changes require a written rationale, prerequisite impact, route
  impact, and an explicit alternative when access or hardware is restrictive.
- Safety objections block publication until resolved or the activity is moved
  behind a clear supervision boundary.
- Translation changes are reviewed for meaning and structure, not literal word
  matching.
- When maintainers disagree, the narrower reversible change is preferred while
  evidence is gathered. The final rationale is recorded in the change history.

一手证据支持的事实纠错优先；课程体系变更必须说明理由、先修影响、路线影响，以及
访问或硬件受限时的替代方案；安全异议在解决或明确转入监督边界前可阻止发布；
翻译按含义与结构评审；有分歧时优先采用范围更小、可逆的变更，并记录最终理由。

## Roles / 角色

- **Contributor:** proposes content, code, research evidence, or corrections.
- **Reviewer:** checks evidence, scope, bilingual parity, safety, and tests.
- **Maintainer:** merges reviewed work, protects release gates, and coordinates
  releases.

Roles are earned through sustained, constructive contributions. No role grants
authority to bypass quality or safety gates. Conflicts of interest must be
disclosed when recommending a course, platform, tool, or product.

角色通过持续、建设性的贡献获得。任何角色都无权绕过质量或安全门禁。推荐课程、
平台、工具或产品时，必须披露利益冲突。

## Releases / 发布

A release requires the checks in [QUALITY.md](QUALITY.md), a reviewed quality
report, and a green remote workflow. Emergency corrections may use a shortened
review cycle, but they still require focused tests and a documented follow-up
review.
