## This Course Starts Where EE 105 Ends

The Berkeley [EE 140 / EE 240A Spring 2025 home page](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~pister/140sp25/)
calls it Analog Integrated Circuits. The shared lectures, homework, labs, and
project assign Razavi's 2nd edition to EE 140 and
Gray/Hurst/Lewis/Meyer's 5th edition to EE 240A; both are separate commercial
texts. The official
[catalog](https://www2.eecs.berkeley.edu/Courses/ELENG140/)
requires EE 105 and lists transistor amplifiers, op-amps, feedback/frequency
response, bias/references, ADCs, switched-capacitor filters, comparators,
hardware laboratory, and design project.

Diagnose readiness on one MOS common-source stage with 3 actions: derive
\(g_m,r_o\), and gain from bias; estimate dominant pole/step response from
capacitances; read loop gain, phase margin, and swing. Review device
regions/models after the 1st failure, Bode poles/zeros after the 2nd, and
feedback/stability after the 3rd; then explain a differential pair's
common-mode limit. Otherwise return to EE 105 or Cornell ECE 3150's solved
homework loop. This is EEDIY placement, not Berkeley enrollment permission.

## Twenty-One Lecture PDFs and Ten Homeworks Form the Off-Campus Analysis Spine

The official Spring 2025 lecture directory lists 21 PDFs: diode/BJT/MOS
physics, small-signal and single-pole
common-source models; active loads, mirrors, differential pairs, 2-stage
op-amps, feedback, stability/compensation, RHP zero, and supply-independent
bias; then telescopic/folded cascodes, switched capacitors, ADC/PGA,
StrongArm, gain boosting, and noise. Each hand estimate feeds the next
schematic, simulation, and review.

The official homework directory contains HW1–HW10, with both the original and
v2 of HW7. The current directory
supplies solutions only for HW8 and HW10, plus Spring 2025 Midterm 2. The home
page links solved exams from older sites, but not all 10 current homeworks are
solved. Attempt HW1–HW10 in order; calibrate with HW8/HW10. Before an old
solution, record term/problem and verify topology and process parameters.

A hand-analysis page records bias region, \(V_{OV}\), small-signal parameters,
gain/poles, swing/common mode, and power. Before resizing after a mismatch,
locate model simplification, schematic error, or PDK behavior. The course page
allows collaboration/debugging but requires original answers/schematics and
citation of old solutions; preserve that boundary.

[Lab 2](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~pister/140sp25/labs/lab2.pdf)

not off-campus reproducibility of Lab 2 or Labs 4–8.

## The Final Project Collects Every Restricted Link in One Mixed-Signal Chip

The official [project specification](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~pister/140sp25/labs/project.pdf)
asks a 3-person analog team to design the analog components of a
battery-powered mixed-signal product: an 8-bit SAR ADC, switched-capacitor
PGA, bandgap and temperature sensor, analog multiplexer, and analog and
digital regulators. The battery range is 3.2 V down to as close to 1.6 V as
possible, at 0–70 °C. Blocks meet at system interfaces. This is the real
Spring 2025 project, not an EEDIY op-amp exercise.

Local specifications conflict: folded-cascode gain/bandwidth enter PGA and
regulator; reference/noise enter ADC; switches/charge injection enter PGA/SAR;
battery droop limits headroom. Trace each specification through hand analysis,
schematic/testbench, corner/temperature sweep, integration waveform, and
decision. The PDF grants neither servers, configured SKY130/Cadence, nor GSI
sign-off; without them, do not mark the project complete.

## Stop Once before Virtuoso, Then Decide Whether to Build a Non-Official Port

According to the official course page, the public route uses 21 lecture PDFs,
HW1–HW10, and solved exams. Complete only Lab 1's hand/LTspice subset and Lab
3 simulations whose input files are actually available; mark physical
measurements and TA/GSI sign-off unexecuted. For unsolved homework, preserve
the derivation, cross-check solved exams, and seek peer review—not restricted
bCourses or Ed. Outside learners receive no classroom grade, staff feedback,
or discussion history.

An “EEDIY open-EDA port” is not Berkeley Lab 2, Labs 4–8, or the project. Choose a
clearly licensed PDK/model/simulator, port one block, and map the original
SKY130/Cadence specification/testbench to it. Publish no instructional files,
commercial models, keys, or server libraries. Until device, corner,
parasitic, noise, and mixed-signal behavior are validated, call it an
alternative implementation, not an equivalent reproduction.

This review found no explicit redistribution permission on the Spring 2025
home page, directories, or lab PDFs. Link originals; do not mirror PDFs or
replace paid texts with unauthorized copies. Public access is not permission.

## Choosing Berkeley Means Choosing a Deep Chain with a Visible Break

Cornell's official ECE 3150 archive lists 12 solved homeworks, solved exams,
and Labs 1–3 solutions: a fuller outside answer loop. EE 140/240A goes deeper
into op-amps, compensation, bias, switched capacitors, ADCs, mixed-signal
integration, and project. Choose Cornell first, then Berkeley when its
prerequisite and Virtuoso/project break are acceptable.

Per the official page and indexes, public evidence is corrected HW1–HW10,
timed scripts for at least 2 solved exams, rerunnable LTspice subsets from
Labs 1/3, and a hand-analysis ledger for 21 PDFs. List the physical/sign-off
parts of Labs 1/3 and Lab 2, Labs 4–8, and the project as read, not executed.
An off-campus port report needs PDK/simulator licenses, versions, block,
testbenches, corners, and discrepancies. That may change the reproducibility
judgment; one waveform cannot.
