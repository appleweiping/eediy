## Course structure

IIT Bombay / NPTEL's [Principles of Digital Communications](https://nptel.ac.in/courses/108101113)
is currently marked Jul–Oct 2026, 12 weeks, and 65 lectures. The
[syllabus](https://archive.nptel.ac.in/content/syllabus_pdf/108101113.pdf)
requires signals and systems plus basic probability/random variables. It
moves from information, source/channel coding, signal space, and AWGN
detection into PCM/DPCM, line coding, pulse shaping/ISI, ASK/PSK/FSK/QPSK/MSK,
and Hamming codes. It fits learners needing a complete derivation chain.
Review probability, convolution, or energy/power signals first if uncertain.

## Control the Breadth with an End-to-End Model

Maintain source symbols → source code → waveform → pulse shaping → channel →
matched filter/statistic → decision → channel decode. At each layer, state
rate, bandwidth, energy normalization, synchronization/CSI, and error metric,
especially distinguishing `Es/N0`, `Eb/N0`, and sampling SNR. The exit should
include Gram–Schmidt and matched-filter equivalence, a raised-cosine eye
diagram, and a small Hamming generator/parity-check/syndrome exercise. Then
compare theoretical BER and Monte Carlo at fixed `Eb/N0`, preserving a
confidence interval and a normalization failure.

## Assignments and feedback

The official archive exposes old
[Assignment 2](https://archive.nptel.ac.in/content/storage2/courses/downloads_new/108101113/noc18_ee27_Assignment2.pdf),
[Assignment 8](https://archive.nptel.ac.in/content/storage2/courses/downloads_new/108101113/noc18_ee27_Assignment8.pdf),
and [Assignment 13](https://archive.nptel.ac.in/content/storage2/courses/downloads_new/108101113/noc18_ee27_Assignment13.pdf).
They belong to `noc18_ee27`; their order, deadlines, and accepted-answer traces
are not Jul–Oct 2026 work or staff feedback. Assignment 13 also does not imply
a 13th course week.

The public material has no reproducible coding lab or hardware experiment.
Entropy/capacity, AWGN detection, pulse-shaping, and modulation-BER notebooks
can be independent numerical supplements, but they are not official NPTEL work
and produce no NPTEL grade. Certification,
current weekly assignments, and a proctored exam depend on current enrollment
and rules and should be recorded separately from public lectures and 2018
problems. In the signal-space block, work through an orthogonal-basis
expansion by hand and explain why Euclidean-distance decisions equal a bank of
matched-filter outputs. In the waveform block, place quantizer overload, ISI,
roll-off, and timing recovery in a single error budget. A modulation comparison
must hold mapping, bits per symbol, coherence assumptions, and code rate fixed
so energy normalization is not mistaken for algorithmic gain. Map old
assignments only to topics actually present in their prompts; an accepted-answer
trace from the old platform still needs an independent derivation or analytic
baseline. Finally, take a specified link from source rate to decision
statistic and BER, and name at least two limits of its model.

The breadth of 65 lectures matters only when source rate, waveform energy,
matched-filter statistics, and BER can all be derived for a specified link
from the same assumptions.
