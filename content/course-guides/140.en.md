## Course fit

MIT **HST.582J Biomedical Signal and Image Processing, Spring 2007** places DSP in cardiology, speech, and medical imaging. Its syllabus centers 5 MATLAB labs. Lab 1 performs ECG filtering and a ventricular-arrhythmia detector, while Lab 3/4 use clinical brain MRI. These are teaching tasks, not diagnostic authorization.

Named prerequisites can be 6.003, 2.004, 16.004, or 18.085. Enter able to explain sampling/aliasing, FIR/IIR stability, DFT leakage/windows, a Bayes two-class boundary, and 2-D covariance/eigenvectors/PCA.

[Lecture Notes](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/hst-582j-biomedical-signal-and-image-processing-spring-2007/pages/lecture-notes/) covers 24 lecture positions, but MRI, surgical applications, random signals III, and wrap-up have no corresponding public note, and some material comes from 1999–2001. This is a historical archive, not a 2026 clinical-practice standard.

## Reorder the course around Lab 0–5

The [Calendar](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/hst-582j-biomedical-signal-and-image-processing-spring-2007/pages/calendar/) interleaves a 4-hour weekly lab. For independent study, use the inputs and outputs from [Labs](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/hst-582j-biomedical-signal-and-image-processing-spring-2007/pages/labs/):

- Optional Lab 0, 1 week: MATLAB tutorial/self-test.
- Lab 1 ECG, 3 weeks, Lectures 1–6: 12 `.txt`, 11 `.mat`, 1 `.m`.
- Lab 2 speech, 3 weeks, Lectures 7–8: 2 `.mat`, 13 `.m`.
- Lab 3 MRI segmentation, 2 weeks, Lectures 9–14: supporting data, 9 `.m`, 2 `.gz`.
- Lab 4 registration, 2 weeks, Lectures 15–17: 1 `.mat`, 22 `.m`.
- Lab 5 BSS, 2 weeks, Lectures 18–20: 10 `.dat`, 7 `.m`.

[Assignments](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/hst-582j-biomedical-signal-and-image-processing-spring-2007/pages/assignments/) has graded Problem Set 1–5 plus X and Y. The original course also had 2 quizzes, but the public page supplies neither quiz papers nor PS solutions.

## Reports matter more than code that merely runs

The official [Syllabus](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/hst-582j-biomedical-signal-and-image-processing-spring-2007/pages/syllabus/) assigns 60% to lab reports and 10% to problem sets. Lab 1 should report threshold-selection/check splits, pre/post spectra, and fixed-threshold counts. Lab 2 connects codec parameters to waveform, spectrogram, and audible artifacts. Lab 3 gives tissue rules, region counts, and overlays. Lab 4 gives transforms, objective traces, and overlays from multiple initializations. Lab 5 places mixture/separated traces together and explains scale, sign, and permutation ambiguity.

Reports should also name algorithm assumptions: stationarity/independence for ECG and BSS, density models for segmentation, and registration sensitivity to interpolation, coordinate direction, and initialization. Listening in Lab 2 only supplements waveform/spectrogram results. A lower Lab 4 objective must return to overlays on the fixed slice. These five interpretations cannot collapse into an overall accuracy.

Repeated tuning on course data is not generalization, and a lower objective does not prove anatomical alignment. Every conclusion remains about the supplied teaching data, not medical-device validation. Keep one instructive failure in each report—an ECG false positive, speech artifact, missed segment, local registration error, or BSS leakage—and connect it to the relevant model assumption.

## Tools and environment

The 2007 course used MATLAB on an MIT Server electronic classroom; the public package lacks that server, TAs, and supervised lab. When migrating to Python/NumPy/SciPy or GNU Octave, preserve the original archive and attribution, then verify array orientation, 1-based indexing, filter initial state, FFT normalization, image coordinates, optimizer tolerance, and seed. Opening a `.mat` or compressed image does not establish numerical equivalence.

If data files are renamed, the manifest must preserve subject and condition identifiers, and third-party medical images require a redistribution check. A migrated figure should use identical input, normalization, and evaluation slice as the old implementation so a compatibility repair does not silently change the experiment. Align the old and new implementation first on a minimal data fragment, then run the complete dataset; this separates migration bugs from limits of the algorithm.

Keep each Lab 1–5 codebase, report, and data license together; Lab 0 is not a formal lab. Do not replace the distinct assumptions of ECG, speech, segmentation, registration, and BSS with a single deep-learning classifier.

## Keep biomedical scope inside offline teaching data

Use only OCW data packages. Attach no human electrode, recruit no volunteer, process no new patient record, and do not relabel assignment accuracy as clinical sensitivity/specificity. Human measurement, identifiable health data, or clinical decisions require institutional ethics, privacy, and professional oversight. A sensible result is 5 recomputable reports and 5 problem-set records, not a diagnostic-performance claim.

The five HST.582J labs share DSP tools but not evaluation criteria: ECG work
asks about events and false positives, speech about coding distortion, and MRI
segmentation, registration, and BSS each rest on different model assumptions.
