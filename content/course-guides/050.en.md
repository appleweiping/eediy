## Prerequisites

MIT 6.374 is a Fall 2003 transistor-level digital-IC course. Its official
[syllabus](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-374-analysis-and-design-of-digital-integrated-circuits-fall-2003/pages/syllabus/)
expects 6.012 or equivalent device physics and 6.004 or equivalent logic
design. Test readiness with one CMOS inverter: draw its VTC and noise margins,
estimate rise/fall delay and transition energy, then explain how layout
parasitics change the answer. Review MOS devices and RC if Verilog is stronger
than transistor analysis; choose a top-down course such as
[Berkeley EECS 151](https://www2.eecs.berkeley.edu/Courses/EECS151/) first if
digital logic is not yet systematic.

The question in 6.374 is not how to write RTL. It is why a CMOS gate,
interconnect, clock, or memory cell is slow or energy-hungry and where layout
breaks the ideal model.

## Course structure

The official [calendar](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-374-analysis-and-design-of-digital-integrated-circuits-fall-2003/pages/calendar/)
lists 24 meetings. It moves from MOS models, inverter VTC, delay, and power
through static, dynamic, and pass-transistor logic; logical effort and
arithmetic; latches, registers, metastability, interconnect, and clocks; then
ROM, SRAM, DRAM, and VLSI test. There is no complete public lecture-video
series, so the useful spine is an account in which each design quantity can
be cross-checked.

For one representative logic path, retain the DC transfer curve, hand
RC/delay, pre-layout SPICE, transistor count, activity/energy assumptions, and
extracted capacitance. That reveals whether a faster topology paid in area,
power, or noise margin.

## Coursework

The [assignments page](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-374-analysis-and-design-of-digital-integrated-circuits-fall-2003/pages/assignments/)
publishes Problem Sets 1–5 with all solutions and FAQs for PS1–PS4; PS3 and
PS5 use 0.25 µm CMOS parameters. Write the model, approximations, and units
before checking solutions, then classify an error as device region, missing
capacitance, critical path, or energy/power definition.

The [exams page](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-374-analysis-and-design-of-digital-integrated-circuits-fall-2003/pages/exams/)
provides the Quiz 1 and Quiz 2 papers without a confirmed solution set. The
public [project guidelines](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-374-analysis-and-design-of-digital-integrated-circuits-fall-2003/3fd5ad1346a5389abc54bd158606a17a_guidelines.pdf)
set a 2-page limit and ask for successful and failed ideas, 3 innovations, and
a simulation path, but do not publish one common circuit assignment. They are
a report format, not a reproducible final-project prompt.

## Tools and environment

The official [tools page](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-374-analysis-and-design-of-digital-integrated-circuits-fall-2003/pages/tools/)
names Magic, HSPICE, and NanoSim; the syllabus also mentions IRSIM. Old
commercial licenses, models, and the 0.25 µm process do not arrive with the
webpage, and their area, delay, and leakage cannot be ranked directly against
a modern PDK.

A practical independent implementation chooses an inverter and one logic path
in a lawfully available, version-pinned modern PDK/EDA flow and compares hand
analysis, pre-layout simulation, DRC-clean layout, extraction, and post-layout
results. Identify it as a modern port and record the PDK, tool, supply,
temperature, and extraction rules. Without a lawful toolchain, the solved
sets and analysis remain a coherent path. The important judgment is where the
model first fails between hand analysis, pre-layout simulation, extraction,
and post-layout—not a numerical comparison between a modern PDK and the 2003
process.
