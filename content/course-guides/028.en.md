## Course Coverage and Who It Suits

Cornell ECE 2100 Spring 2019 puts DC, transient and sinusoidal circuits,
op-amps and transistors, a switching converter, Laplace transforms and
transfer functions, filters, complex power, and transformers in a coherent course.
The official [course details](https://ocw.ece.cornell.edu/ece-2100-course-details/)
also require MATLAB and laboratory instruments. Choose it for a broad,
substantial undergraduate circuits course when you have access to a safe bench.
The assigned textbook is commercial, and the public release is incomplete.

## Three Lectures and Every Solution Are Missing

The [lecture notes](https://ocw.ece.cornell.edu/ece-2100-lecture-notes/)
publish Lectures 1–22, 25–27, and 29–40: 37 sets in total. Lectures 23, 24,
and 28 are absent. Use the outline or textbook to identify the missing topic
before continuing across a number gap.

The [homework and labs](https://ocw.ece.cornell.edu/ece-2100-homework-and-labs/)
provide Homework 1–6 and Lab/Prelab 1–6, but no homework or lab solutions and
no exams. Check problems with KCL/KVL residuals, power balance, limiting cases,
and independent simulation. For each lab, preserve the prelab → prediction →
build/measure → report order, including the schematic, measured part values,
instrument settings, raw waveform, and a debugging episode. Simulation-only
work reaches the theory and prelab, not the original laboratory outcome.

Before touching the bench, write what each instrument should show if the model
is correct. When data disagree, inspect the supply, ground, probe ratio, source
impedance, component values, and test point before changing the model. A
preserved failed measurement often demonstrates more analytical skill than a
polished final curve.

## Check Every Interface in a Sensor-to-ADC Front End

Finish with a low-voltage sensor input → op-amp/filter → ADC-compatible output.
Specify source impedance, supply, gain and bandwidth, load, noise, and error
before measuring each stage and the end-to-end response. The release is strong
because of its 37 note sets and complete prelab/lab documents, but its 3
missing lectures, commercial text, absent solutions, and absent exams are
real gaps. This small design reveals whether passive, transient,
frequency-domain, and active circuits form a coherent course. Choose the
archive for its unusually concrete lab documents; at the missing lectures and
unsolved work, rely on the text, limiting cases, and measurements together.
