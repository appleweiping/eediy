## Course fit

IIT Kanpur / NPTEL [Basic Overview of Semiconductor Device Processing and IC Fabrication](https://nptel.ac.in/courses/108104865) is taught by S. Sundar Kumar Iyer. This page follows the Jan–Apr 2026 run: 12 weeks and 39 lectures. The [cohort preview](https://onlinecourses.nptel.ac.in/noc26_ee04/preview) identifies that run, while the [official syllabus](https://archive.nptel.ac.in/content/syllabus_pdf/108104865.pdf) anchors the topics. Enrollment and exam details need to be checked again for later cohorts.

The official listing gives no formal prerequisite, but the overview becomes a vocabulary list if MOS capacitors, diodes, MOSFETs, crystals, and doping are still unfamiliar. Before starting, be able to sketch a planar n+-p diode and simplified CMOS cross-section and distinguish nm, µm, cm⁻³, dose/cm², and sheet resistance.

## Read the process through one cross-section

The official syllabus places IC history and scaling, planar diodes, CMOS mask flow, and advanced FETs in the first 3 weeks. Packaging, vacuum, plasma, substrates, and the choice among silicon, SOI, and GaAs follow. Oxidation, diffusion, and ion implantation in Weeks 7–9 are the center of the course because they connect thermal budget, junction depth, dopant profile, and lateral spread. CVD/PVD, lithography, and etching then determine whether those structures can actually be fabricated.

Keep returning to the same CMOS cross-section. After each unit process, mark what was added, removed, or doped and ask how later heating changes an existing junction. The integration tradeoffs then become visible: a deeper drive-in may reduce a local resistance while increasing junction depth; a more aggressive etch may open a window while damaging the film below. That causal story is more useful than memorizing an equipment list.

## A paper process project

The public description mentions practical aspects of fabrication laboratories, but it provides no open equipment, approved recipe, wafer traveler, raw measurement, or graded project. A suitable independent substitute is a paper flow for a planar diode or simplified CMOS cell: draw the mask list, sequential cross-sections, and thermal budget, then choose oxide thickness, implant dose, or etch bias and explain its effect on junction capacitance, sheet resistance, or threshold.

Label every number as course-derived, externally sourced, or a teaching assumption. One well-explained case where a locally better unit process makes the integrated flow worse is more valuable than a polished cross-section with no process origin.

## Laboratory boundary

See the [Laboratory Safety guide](../../guides/safety.md) for general cleanroom stop conditions. This course can map the relationships among substrate, thermal history, junctions, films, lithography, packaging, and electrical metrics. It does not qualify anyone to operate cleanroom equipment. Any paper flow entering a real facility remains subject to local design rules, approved recipes, and equipment-compatibility review.
