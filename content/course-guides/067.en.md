## 6.302 begins when the Bode plot is connected to a motor

MIT 6.302 is best for someone who knows LTI and frequency-domain analysis but has not yet shaped a classical loop around a real plant. Its [syllabus](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-302-feedback-systems-spring-2007/pages/syllabus/) names 6.003, 2.003, 16.04, or equivalent preparation and assumes circuits, physics, mathematics, and prior circuit-laboratory experience. Servomotors and op-amps bring return ratio, root locus, Nyquist, Hall/Nichols, Bode plots, lead and lag, and minor-loop compensation into one feedback loop.

Use 6.241J instead if state space is the goal. If \(T=L/(1+L)\) remains a memorized expression, repair Laplace transforms, block diagrams, and LTI first. A workable starting point is the ability to derive a low-order model from torque or circuit equations and identify where reference, disturbance, measurement noise, and control effort enter.

## Access and version notes

The [Calendar](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-302-feedback-systems-spring-2007/pages/calendar/) lists L1–L24 and R1–R26, moving from desensitivity, modeling, and transient response to Nyquist, Bode margins, loop shaping, oscillation, and conditional stability. [Problem Set 1–10](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-302-feedback-systems-spring-2007/pages/assignments/) contains questions only. [Recitations](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-302-feedback-systems-spring-2007/pages/recitations/) actually offers 24 handouts, with R21 and R22 missing.

The [Labs](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-302-feedback-systems-spring-2007/pages/labs/) reveal the course design. One servomotor lasts through 1A–1D: measure static gain and time constant, build an open-loop model, add compensation, then compare predicted and observed response. After that, select only 1 Lab 2 project from PLL, thermal control, and magnetic levitation. These are not four unrelated rigs; one model keeps meeting new evidence.

## Evaluation criteria

For a second-order motor or an op-amp with a minor loop, state the operating point, units, and omitted poles, delay, friction, and saturation. Apply root locus and Nyquist under one sign convention, then allocate tracking, disturbance rejection, sensor noise, control effort, and robustness to frequency bands. Each lead, lag, or minor loop should have a named benefit and cost.

[python-control](https://github.com/python-control/python-control) is an active BSD-3-Clause library covering state space, Bode, Nyquist, root locus, and time response. It makes a useful second implementation of the same nominal and perturbed plant under the same signs, with one margin checked at a hand-calculated frequency. Saturation still lies outside the LTI model; a smooth software step does not replace actuator voltage/current and slew-rate checks.

The problem sets have no answers, so dimensions, limiting cases, and a second stability test must cross-check the derivation. [Exams](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-302-feedback-systems-spring-2007/pages/exams/) includes the 2003 Quiz 1 and finals from 2003, 2004, 2005, and 2007, all unsolved. Figures are missing from 2005 Final Problem 4, so its conditions cannot be reconstructed honestly.

## Labs and projects

The PLL route asks about lock range, capture behavior, and noise. Thermal control brings heater power, sensor lag, and a temperature ceiling. Maglev requires constrained motion, coil-current limits, flyback protection, and a safe de-energized state. Physical work needs low-voltage current limiting, guards for rotation or heat and coils, and an independent shutdown. Otherwise compare nominal and perturbed plants in MATLAB/Octave or Python-control.

End by putting nominal success beside failure: a little more gain makes delay drive oscillation; a faster command pushes the actuator into saturation and invalidates the linear settling-time prediction; friction or dead zone prevents repeated steps from agreeing. The durable lesson of 6.302 is not how to polish margins, but how to identify the first unmodeled effect that makes those margins stop explaining the plant.
