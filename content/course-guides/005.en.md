## A Complex-Variables Course That Transfers Well to EE

MIT 18.04 Spring 2018 is unusually coherent: the official
[syllabus](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-04-complex-variables-with-applications-spring-2018/pages/syllabus/)
aligns 37 [lecture blocks](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-04-complex-variables-with-applications-spring-2018/pages/lecture-notes/)
with residues, conformal maps, Laplace transforms, and analytic continuation.
There are no public lecture videos. Choose it if you are willing to read
derivations and solve problems, especially if you want firmer mathematics
behind frequency-domain methods, planar fields, or boundary-value
problems. It is excessive if you only need a quick integral-reference sheet.

The formal prerequisite is 18.02 and 18.03, or equivalent 18.032 material.
Before starting, be comfortable with line integrals, constant-coefficient
differential equations, complex exponentials, and multivalued arguments.
Without the latter skills it is possible to imitate residue calculations while
continually mishandling branches, annuli of convergence, and contour choices.

## Problems Matter More Than a Substitute Video Series

Read a short block of notes, attempt its examples, and then inspect the
[recitations](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-04-complex-variables-with-applications-spring-2018/pages/recitations/)
for the instructor's contour and Laurent-region decisions. All 9
[problem sets](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-04-complex-variables-with-applications-spring-2018/pages/problem-sets-and-solutions/)
have solutions. Finish your own derivation first, then classify discrepancies
as algebra errors, missing hypotheses, or poor strategy.

State the branch and branch cut for a complex logarithm, the center and annulus
for every Laurent series, and all enclosed singularities before using the
residue theorem. Also explain why any auxiliary arc vanishes. Those conditions
show more understanding than the final number.

## End with an Unseen Residue or Conformal-Mapping Problem

The [exam archive](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-04-complex-variables-with-applications-spring-2018/pages/exams/)
contains practice papers, course papers, and solutions for closed-book checks.
Then choose one transfer problem: derive an inverse transform with residues or
solve a planar field boundary with a conformal map, and
state the valid region, singularities, and failure conditions. Being able to
defend each analytic step is a better outcome than remembering a familiar
contour picture.
