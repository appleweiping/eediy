## Grid and machine courses share per-unit language but retain separate spines

The [official page](https://nptel.ac.in/courses/117105140) for [NPTEL Power System Analysis](118-117105140.md) covers per-unit quantities, power flow, faults, and stability. Video and assignments are open, certification is paid, and there is no public code-to-laboratory loop. The [official OCW archive](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-691-seminar-in-electric-power-systems-spring-2006/) for [MIT 6.691](119-6-691.md) emphasizes operation, planning, and markets. Its 7 solved sets, small MATLAB programs, and project samples are valuable, but the rules and cases belong to 2006.

On the machine side, the [official syllabus](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-685-electric-machines-fall-2013/pages/syllabus/) for [MIT 6.685](120-6-685.md) develops electromechanical conversion and machine models through notes and 11 problem sets; its public archive omits the scheduled 3-hour final. The [official NPTEL page](https://nptel.ac.in/courses/108105131) for [Electrical Machines II](121-108105131.md) provides a video alternative but no reproducible experiment or feedback chain. Static power flow does not require a complete machine course, and single-machine dynamics does not require a market course. Select one from each side only when a grid-connected machine is the actual question.

## Read 6.691 as an archive of 2006 cases

The market rules, load data, and blackout discussions in 6.691 train modeling and argument, but they do not directly support a 2026 operational claim. Current statements about ISO or RTO dispatch, reserves, or transmission rules need present primary data from an ISO, RTO, regulator, or utility with time zone, base, revision, and license stated beside the 2006 case. Small course MATLAB programs explain the original computational task. A migration to pandapower, OpenDSS, Python, or GNU Octave should map the dataset, solver, tolerance, and model explicitly.

Similarly, the missing 6.685 final should not be assembled into an official exam from third-party answers, and open NPTEL assignments do not imply an anonymous grader. A historical archive is valuable because its original prompts, data, and context can be identified, not because it can be presented as a current grid or industry workflow. For the same case, write the “2006 course conclusion” separately from the “current-data recheck” so their time conditions do not become mixed.

## Make a two-bus power flow initialize the synchronous-machine swing equation

Three-phase complex power and network equations from [circuit analysis](../circuits/index.md), magnetic circuits, air-gap fields, induction, and torque from [electromagnetics](../electromagnetics/index.md), and nonlinear solving and differential equations from [engineering mathematics](../mathematics/index.md) meet here. Choose \(S_\mathrm{base}\) and voltage bases for a two-bus case, perform per-unit conversion, solve the flow, and inspect power balance and Newton-Raphson mismatch. Map bus-voltage phasor and terminal power into internal emf, reactance, rotor angle, mechanical power, and swing-equation initial state. In a short undisturbed run, rotor speed stays synchronous, angle derivative stays near zero, and electric-mechanical power difference matches the declared loss.

Use one base, sign, and convention sheet for paper and source, explicitly stating line versus phase, RMS versus peak, generator and load signs, sequence components, and torque direction. A solver's converged status still requires checks of bus residuals, generator reactive and voltage limits, and the Jacobian. Electrical power, mechanical power, and speed at dynamic time zero must agree with steady state. If a fault changes topology, give the pre-fault, faulted, and post-fault admittance matrices and preserve state continuity at clearing.

## Send one disturbance through network limits, torque, and rotor angle

The grid branch builds a small multi-bus case with explicit load, generation, line, and voltage limits. After meeting a chosen residual tolerance, apply an N-1 event, balanced or unbalanced fault, or clearing-time sweep. The machine branch connects a synchronous or induction machine to a Thevenin source and mechanical load, calculates torque, flux, speed, and loss, and runs a startup or load transient. When combined, pass the same power-flow operating point into the machine model and plot how the network event changes electromagnetic torque and rotor state.

Physical three-phase supplies, machines, transformers, and fault rigs combine mains voltage, starting current, rotating energy, exposed terminals, and entanglement hazards and belong in a protected, supervised laboratory. Outside work defaults to simulation or public data. The report includes provenance, per-unit bases, a network or machine diagram, equations, source, solver release, raw output, and a hand-checkable case. It also shows a parameter point with non-convergence, a reactive limit violation, loss of synchronism, or a thermal limit crossing. Refine a clearing-time sweep near the transition so stable/unstable classification is not set by a coarse grid. Center the conclusion on the first constraint crossing and associated energy flow.
